# Acme Co. Customer Policies

## Refunds

Acme Co. offers a full refund on any unopened product returned within 30 days of
the original purchase date. To request a refund, contact support@acme.example
with your order number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
typically appear within 5–10 business days.

Opened products may be eligible for a partial refund (up to 50% of the purchase
price) at our discretion, provided they are returned in resaleable condition.
Digital products, gift cards, and items marked "final sale" are not refundable.

If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 7 days of delivery. We will
replace the item or issue a full refund — including return shipping — at no
cost to the customer.

## Shipping

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time on a business day are dispatched the
same day. Standard shipping arrives in 3–5 business days within the contiguous
United States. Expedited shipping (1–2 business days) is available at checkout
for an additional fee.

Acme Co. ships to most international destinations. International orders may
incur customs duties or import taxes, which are the responsibility of the
recipient. International delivery times vary between 7 and 21 business days
depending on destination and customs processing.

We do not currently ship to PO boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or freight forwarders.

## Warranty

All Acme Co. products carry a limited one-year warranty against manufacturing
defects, beginning on the date of delivery. The warranty does not cover damage
from misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications. To file a warranty
claim, email warranty@acme.example with your order number and a description of
the issue. Approved claims result in repair, replacement, or store credit at
Acme's discretion.
